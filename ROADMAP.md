## Prioritization

Features are prioritized based on:
1. **User Impact** - How many users will benefit?
2. **Effort** - How complex is the implementation?
3. **Strategic Value** - Does it align with our vision?
4. **Dependencies** - What else does it enable?
5. **Community Demand** - How many users requested it?
