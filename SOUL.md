# SOUL.md - The Spirit of QwenCore

## Our Purpose

QwenCore exists to help people focus, work smarter, and achieve their goals through the proven Pomodoro Technique. We believe that structured work sessions with intentional breaks lead to better productivity, creativity, and well-being.

## Core Values

### 🎯 Focus First
We believe in the power of deep, uninterrupted focus. Every feature we build helps users concentrate better and work more effectively.

### 🌱 Sustainable Productivity
Burnout is real. We design for sustainable work patterns that respect human energy cycles and encourage regular rest.

### 🔒 Privacy by Design
Your data belongs to you. Period. We store everything locally. No tracking. No analytics. No compromises.

### ✨ Simplicity
Complexity is the enemy of execution. We strive for elegant simplicity in both design and code.

### 🎨 Beauty Matters
A beautiful tool is a joy to use. We care deeply about aesthetics, animations, and the little details that make software delightful.

### 🌍 Universal Access
Productivity tools should be accessible to everyone, regardless of device, ability, or technical expertise.

## Design Philosophy

### Minimalism
Remove everything that doesn't serve the user's focus. Every pixel, every interaction, every feature must earn its place.

### Clarity
Users should never wonder "what does this do?" or "where am I?" The interface should be self-explanatory and intuitive.

### Delight
Small moments of joy matter. The satisfying click of a button, the smooth animation of a transition, the gentle chime of a completed session.

### Trust
Users trust us with their time and focus. We honor that trust through reliability, consistency, and respect for their data.

## The Pomodoro Philosophy

The Pomodoro Technique isn't just about timers. It's about:

1. **Awareness** - Understanding how you spend your time
2. **Intention** - Choosing what to focus on
3. **Rhythm** - Working in harmony with your natural energy cycles
4. **Rest** - Recognizing that breaks are productive
5. **Progress** - Measuring improvement over time
6. **Balance** - Finding sustainable work patterns
